WHAT IS ETHOSQUEST?

EthosQuest is an interactive scenario-based learning system designed to help students practice disaster preparedness and ethical decision-making through realistic fictional situations.

Rather than simply reading what they should do, students are placed in situations where they must think, choose, respond, and experience the possible consequences of their decisions.

THINK. CHOOSE. RESPOND. LEARN.
